            Strategy Tips

            • Balance RCI zones — residents need jobs and shops nearby.
            • Connect power plants to zones with power lines.
            • Roads must touch zone edges for development.
            • Spread zones to reduce disaster damage concentration.
            • Trade-off: industrial growth vs. resilience and sustainability.
            • Harm index is 0–100 (higher is worse). It rises with people killed, injuries, damaged zones, destroyed roads, and disaster cost.
            • During a class, use Lab sheet to record Design A and Design B. The lecturer sends the disaster.
            • Outside a class, disasters can strike at random, or you can trigger one from the Disaster panel. Every event is listed in the disaster log.

            Settings

            When enabled, you see a welcome prompt each visit until you finish or skip the tour. Use Replay Guided Tour anytime.

            City

            Cities download as a .json file. A short code like X12345 cannot hold a 16×16 map, and it would be guessable. A restore slip (s123456-K7M2) is a random token stored on the server for your HSU ID only. There is no autosave. Importing someone else’s file is practice only and will not update the leaderboard. The class timer always keeps wall-clock time.

            Reset wipes the current map. This cannot be undone.
