Classroom SimCity

Log in with your HSU ID. Do not use a classmate’s ID. Scores stay on that ID, and the teacher can see last login. Teachers: use admin.

First time this semester?

Claim your ID once with the board code the teacher writes in class. After that, only your personal lock works — classmates cannot guess a city from a short code, and they cannot log in as you without your lock.

Forgot your lock? Ask the teacher to reset it in Admin. There is no email reset.
